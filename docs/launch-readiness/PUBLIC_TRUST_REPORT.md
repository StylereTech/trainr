## Findings And Changes

**High severity:** public profiles displayed unconditional identity/background-verification badges, including for a fixture with no credentials. Marketing and safety pages represented universal screening without a corresponding enforced approval requirement. An uploaded background-check asset or an approved listing is not evidence of universal screening. Removed blanket claims, linked safety guidance, and display each credential's recorded verified/unverified status. This does not independently validate previously marked credentials or certify any trainer's safety.

Removed unsupported hard-coded homepage counts, average rating and testimonials presented as real parent evidence. Removed selected profile/card/footer design commentary. Removed unverified safety-response time guarantees. Updated safety guidance to distinguish listing approval from identity/background screening, and improved its heading/date contrast. Existing safety mailbox delivery and actual escalation ownership remain unverified and require owner confirmation; this is not a legal review or a completed screening program.

**Medium severity:** screenshot inspection found the mobile five-tab profile navigation overflowing its fixed-height container into the About panel. Added a bounding-box regression first: 390px test failed with tab bottom `1605` exceeding container bottom `1565`. Both canonical and legacy profiles now allow the tab container to grow with its rows. No global tab style changed.
